# OpenTrail funding packet

Status date: 2026-08-10

This directory contains reusable, evidence-backed source material for grants,
hardware donations, sponsorship inquiries, and future public funding. It is
written in Markdown so changes remain visible and reviewable on GitHub.

## Naming rule

- **Public product name:** not selected. Use `[PUBLIC PROJECT NAME]` in an
  application until a name is chosen and cleared.
- **Engineering/repository name:** OpenTrail.
- **Umbrella identity and domain:** Limited Underground / `limitedunderground.com`.
- **Legal applicant and payee:** to be confirmed before any submission that
  requires an incorporated entity, tax status, bank information, or fiscal
  sponsor.
- **Funding status:** all cash, hardware, discount, loan, sponsorship, service-
  credit, and other support requests are paused by owner direction. Preserve the
  packet as preparation material only. Do not research for outreach, tailor,
  solicit, submit, email, accept terms or property, direct shipment, open or
  connect an account, or announce support until the hold is explicitly cleared.
- ECLU is not assumed by any funding document, protocol, stored identifier,
  device identity, URL, or hardware marking.

## Packet contents

1. [Project fact sheet](PROJECT_FACT_SHEET.md) — the truthful core description
   and current evidence.
2. [Application answer bank](APPLICATION_ANSWER_BANK.md) — reusable short and
   long answers for common grant questions.
3. [Hardware support request](HARDWARE_SUPPORT_REQUEST.md) — a vendor-neutral
   request for donated or loaned equipment.
4. [Budget and milestones](BUDGET_AND_MILESTONES.md) — planning ranges and
   milestone-based funding levels.
5. [Application checklist](APPLICATION_CHECKLIST.md) — submission gate and
   evidence checklist.
6. [Opportunity register](OPPORTUNITY_REGISTER.md) — dated lead tracking without
   presenting unverified programs as open.

## Truthfulness boundary

Every application must distinguish among:

- documented architecture and host-tested components;
- bounded physical MeshCore bench evidence;
- planned but blocked four-person field testing; and
- future production firmware, security, hardware support, and server features.

Do not describe OpenTrail as production-ready, field-validated, guaranteed
emergency communication, a replacement for regulated safety equipment, or a
supported commercial product. Do not claim that a donated board is compatible
until the repository contains repeatable evidence.

## Submission workflow

1. Copy the relevant source text into an opportunity-specific draft.
2. Record the opportunity URL, deadline, eligibility rules, match requirement,
   reporting obligations, and permitted expenses in the register.
3. Replace every bracketed field and remove irrelevant sections.
4. Recheck all measurements against current public evidence.
5. Freeze the requested budget with dated quotes.
6. Confirm the legal applicant, authorized signer, payment method, and any tax
   or conflict disclosures.
7. Confirm that the owner has explicitly cleared the overall funding/outreach
   hold and any private legal/financial hold for the exact request.
8. Obtain owner approval before submitting, emailing, accepting terms, taking
   shipment, or connecting financial accounts.
