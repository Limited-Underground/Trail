# Funding and hardware opportunity register

Status date: 2026-08-10

This register separates ideas from verified-open opportunities. A listing is not
an endorsement, an eligibility claim, or authority to submit. Verify every lead
on the official program site on the date of action.

## Status vocabulary

- `lead` — possible source; current terms not yet verified.
- `researching` — official material is under review.
- `qualified` — current eligibility and fit documented.
- `drafting` — opportunity-specific application in progress.
- `approval-needed` — complete draft awaits owner authorization.
- `on-hold` — potentially suitable, but no solicitation, submission, acceptance,
  account connection, or shipment is permitted until the recorded private hold
  is cleared.
- `submitted` — externally submitted with confirmation recorded privately.
- `declined`, `awarded`, `closed`, or `not-a-fit` — terminal status with reason.
