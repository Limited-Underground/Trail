# Funding budget and milestone model

Status date: 2026-08-10

These are planning ranges, not quotes or an approved spending authorization.
Every application must replace them with dated, opportunity-eligible prices and
identify tax, shipping, donated value, matching funds, and restricted costs.

All cash, hardware, discount, loan, sponsorship, and service-credit requests are
currently on hold. These ranges remain planning material only and do not
authorize opportunity research, outreach, submission, account activity,
acceptance, shipment, or purchasing.

## First funded outcome

Produce four identical self-contained client units plus two spares, complete the
minimum direct target integration, and conduct the defined four-person pilot in
at least three materially different one-hour environments.

## Funding levels

### Level A — in-kind hardware

Requested outcome: six matched candidate nodes and relevant accessories.

Current authorization: on hold.

Acceptance evidence:

- receiving inventory and estimated fair value;
- support terms and conflicts disclosed;
- shipping firmware preserved before any write;
- candidate/experimented/validated status based only on repeatable tests; and
- unused or loaned property tracked through return or disposition.

### Level B — microgrant, approximately $2,500

Requested outcome: close the minimum hardware gap and finish four-unit bench
readiness. This amount may require donated boards or owner cost sharing.

Current authorization: on hold.

Milestone evidence:

- exact bill of materials;
- four working identical units;
- reproducible build/flash/recovery procedure;
- measured GNSS, display/input, power, and radio smoke tests; and
- public expenditure ledger.

### Level C — pilot grant, approximately $5,000–$7,500

Requested outcome: complete Level B plus at least three four-person standalone
field sessions and their public aggregate reports.

Current authorization: on hold.

Milestone evidence:

- all Level B deliverables;
- frozen privacy-safe pilot plan;
- complete session logs and deterministic verdicts;
- participant usability and failure summaries;
- defect/limitation register; and
- next four-plus-repeater plan.

### Level D — extended development, approximately $10,000–$25,000

Requested outcome: repeat the standalone pilot, add the controlled repeater
phase, prepare eight-unit evidence, and prototype the optional server/web path.

This level requires a separately approved scope, labor model, data-retention
policy, security review, and legal applicant. It must not be presented as the
cost of completing or commercially launching the entire system.

Current authorization: on hold.

## Milestone release rule

Whenever possible, request milestone-based release rather than promising a
calendar-only result:

1. hardware and toolchain freeze;
2. four-unit bench acceptance;
3. first four-person session;
4. remaining two environments and final pilot report; and
5. repeater-phase go/no-go decision.

Failed measurements still qualify as useful deliverables when the agreed test
was executed correctly, evidence is complete, funds are accounted for, and the
result is reported honestly.

## Public funding ledger fields

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Receipt, commitment, purchase, refund, or disposition date |
| Source | Funder/vendor, anonymized only when terms permit and privacy requires |
| Type | Cash, discount, donated equipment, loan, credit, or owner contribution |
| Restriction | Eligible purpose, deadline, reporting, return, or publicity terms |
| Estimated value | Currency and valuation basis |
| Use | Item, quantity, milestone, and receipt reference |
| Status | Committed, received, spent, refunded, returned, or remaining |
| Evidence link | Public report, invoice hash/reference, or private controlled record |

Never publish banking information, tax IDs, home addresses, signatures, private
receipts, serial numbers, access tokens, or participant-identifying details.
