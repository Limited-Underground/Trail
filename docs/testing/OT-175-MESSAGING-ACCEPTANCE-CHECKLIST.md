## Stop and acceptance rules

Stop the affected case on wrong recipient, privacy leak, unplanned transmit, loss of recovery readiness, unknown identity, or misleading delivery state. Preserve sanitized evidence and record **FAIL**, **BLOCKED** (missing prerequisite) or **NOT RUN**. None counts as PASS. Do not retry outside the approved plan. Checklist review checks traceability and measurable evidence; parent acceptance separately requires the real results and owner review.
