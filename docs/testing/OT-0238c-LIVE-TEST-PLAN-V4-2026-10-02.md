  field is not a failure of this final screen check.

## Timing and human checkpoints

Propose one common absolute execute/capture ceiling 35 minutes after fresh
issuance and restoration ceiling 60 minutes after issuance, allocating at least
25 minutes for cleanup. Candidate admission may only reduce those ceilings.
Retain the existing operator handoff gate: at least 900 seconds of execution
authority must remain when handing the current originals to the candidate.
Otherwise release originals without candidate execution.

These limits require fresh authorization. The previous trial had 45/60-minute
ceilings; its allocated 15-minute cleanup reserve did not complete B restoration.
Only the later, separately authorized recovery had a common 30-minute ceiling.
The larger reserve is precautionary and remains unmeasured with v4; it does not
guarantee cleanup will finish. No previous grant or crypto deadline is extended.

BEGIN preparation stays at most 120 seconds. Signed invitation and activation
checks remain independently enforced; activation is at most 60 seconds and
shares remaining preparation time. Identity review happens before invitation
but consumes preparation. No new per-poll/human timeout or clock resampling
changes a rejection. Rehearsal precedes BEGIN, not a rushed explanation during
it. If the operator cannot confidently perform the comparisons in those fixed
windows, do not start the physical attempt; revise the workflow separately.

Source/fixture inventory: acquisition 26 worker calls, held freshness 12,
candidate recapture before first write 32 (source-derived). v3 used 74/36/80.
They are not measured physical durations. SDK/USB transfers, firmware startup,
human comparisons and restoration still have unmeasured timing. An hour is an
authority ceiling, not a guarantee of successful enrollment.

The maintained private view requires explicit fresh one-use checkpoint
ACKs. Own-page review compares all four identity rows on both boards against
their own references. Each peer review uses the other board's already checked
reference, not a vanished own page. The last comparison checks full codes on
both boards and requires the one-second BOOT hold/release on each. Do not hold
BOOT ten seconds: that is the reset gesture and is excluded here. Do not press
the other hardware reset button during this case.

## Failure evidence and recovery

Reviewed owned adapter/clock/deadline failures retain their specific fixed
category. Unknown native/SDK failures remain rom_operation_failed or the owning
generic category; raw SDK text, identifiers, payloads and comparison values are
not exported. Record the actual failure and cleanup receipts, not a guessed
cause or a later-clock inference. The old install_B failure remains unknown.

Cancel/window close/refusal leads to checked restoration, not trial replay.
Unknown write/reset/handle completion or pending/torn journals bars automatic
retry. Preserve custody; separately review and authorize a fresh recovery-only
operation if normal cleanup cannot finish. A valid previous recovery is evidence
that its old custody closed, not permission for another recovery.

If setup fails before the trial controller starts, it cannot issue its final
usual-screen checkpoint even if byte restoration/custody closure passes.
Collect a separate owner report of both usual screens in that case; do not
invent a private ACK or equate restored bytes with a physical screen observation.
