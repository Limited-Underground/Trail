# OT-017I Live OpenGauge State Across Physical Retry Lifecycle

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: passed for two role-reversed four-leg close-bench sequences. A single
OpenGauge process owned the real authorization registry, ACK replay ingress, and
outbox before the first radio send and kept them alive until final completion.
This is live host state, not durable restart recovery or on-device firmware.

## Sequence

The live process began with exactly one in-flight normative alert. After the
physical rate-limit rejection it recorded retry release, enforced not-ready at
25 ms, prepared the same frame at 26 ms, and returned to one in-flight entry
before the harness sent the physical retry. The next-sequence accepted ACK then
completed that same live lifecycle.
