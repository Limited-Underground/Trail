GNSS current-fix evidence obtained, clock and loss/recovery acceptance open.

## Observations

On the unchanged original image, the owner had reported `GPS:--` and
`GPS UNKNOWN` even under open sky. Source review showed those lines were
defaults in the normal OLED path, so the original screen could not indicate
receiver state.

On the corrected candidate, the owner first read `GPS:6` and `GPS FIX` with
approximately 84% battery. More than two minutes later, the owner read
`GPS:10` and `GPS FIX` with approximately 85% battery. These observations
establish two current satellite-derived fix presentations, not position
accuracy or sustained fix through arbitrary conditions.

A bounded passive USB capture, with no raw NMEA or coordinates retained,
observed 54,493 GNSS UART bytes, 89 accepted GGA/GNS observations, 981
unsupported-or-rejected sentences, and zero UART read errors. The rejection
counter includes ordinary unsupported NMEA sentence types, so it is not a
fault count. Combined with the visible fixes, the capture confirms that the
receiver, UART path and parser were active during this trial.

The candidate clock stayed `--:--`. The owner reported that V1-Test on the S24
Ultra had trouble reconnecting to the candidate after its reboot. Therefore
the two-reading `clock_advanced` result is false; the observation does not
establish a clock defect or successful sustained clock behavior. After exact
restoration, the owner confirmed the normal Trail screen but initially still
could not connect the S24. Both phones appeared to show fresh pairing, but the
S24's reported `Scan complete` / `No compatible Trail device was found` came
from the separate Add Device search, which excludes an already owned device.
On 2026-09-27, read-only ADB inspection found the V1-Test installation and
existing log intact, plus one Android Bluetooth bond associated with V1-Test.
One clean app restart followed by its ordinary `Find device` -> `Start Bluetooth
device service` route discovered Bench 2, established GATT, accepted protected
ProtocolInfo, MTU, subscription, authorization and Snapshot, and reached Ready.
The screen showed `Connected to Trail Bench 2` and `Display clock synchronized.`
The owner then confirmed the restored original's physical screen displayed a
clock time instead of `--:--`. No new pairing or device reset occurred. This
restored-original result does not retroactively pass the temporary candidate's
sustained-clock test.

The owner also reported that the Note20 connection works again. That is an
owner-observed second-phone outcome, not an ADB trace or a new two-phone
acceptance run; its exact session stages were not independently captured here.

## Second bounded attempt, 2026-09-27

The owner authorized one new Bench 2 candidate trial with the same exact
591456-byte image and a new one-use grant. The revised operator independently
identified the sole connected ESP32-S3 as `OT-DEV-002`, captured the current
original application, NVS, bootloader, partition and OTA spans, verified the
candidate write/readback and boot, then stopped at its new phone-ready gate.
The S24 V1-Test was on the Add Device scan and did not establish the saved-owner
protected session or make a clock visible. The operator recorded
`connection_not_ready`, performed no timed GNSS/clock observation, restored the
captured original application and NVS, read back all five spans, reset the unit,
and closed its journal. An independent host check found the journal closed,
the last event `restored_and_reset`, all five private capture hashes matched,
and no active custody lock. The user was asked to confirm the physical normal
screen; that visual check remains separate from the operator result.

During restoration, ADB read the S24's actual V1-Test screen. It showed
`Scan complete` / `No compatible Trail device was found` in Add Device. A clean
V1-Test restart without clearing app data or pairing returned to `Find device`.
Selecting it showed `Start Bluetooth device service`; selecting that showed
`Checking for authorized device`, then `Connecting` to `Authorized device 1`,
then `Connected to Trail Bench 2` and `Display clock synchronized` on the
restored original. This directly establishes that the returning-owner route
works and the Add Device scan was the wrong route for this already owned unit.
It does not establish the candidate's sustained clock behavior. The next
candidate attempt must use this directly observed saved-owner route while the
operator waits at its clock checkpoint, under a fresh one-use authorization.

## Third bounded attempt, 2026-09-27

The owner authorized one Bench 2-only retry with the same candidate image and
direct S24 ADB inspection during a ten-minute saved-owner connection window.
The controller independently selected Bench 2, captured its application, NVS,
bootloader, partition and OTA spans, verified the candidate write/readback,
and booted it. On the candidate the owner read `GPS:11` and `GPS FIX` with
approximately 33% battery; the clock remained `--:--`. This is another
current-fix presentation, not a sustained-clock pass.

The S24 V1-Test saved-owner route discovered the candidate and established a
GATT link. Its sanitized connection trace then recorded
`PROTECTED_PROTOCOL_INFO_REQUESTED` followed approximately 5.1 seconds later
by `PROTECTED_PROTOCOL_INFO_REJECTED` / `BLE-GATT-PLATFORM-FAILURE`. A clean app
restart without clearing data or pairing repeated that boundary. The label
identifies the failed phase, not proof that the device explicitly rejected the
read or that GNSS code caused the failure. The same phone and route had reached
protected Ready and synchronized the clock on the restored original before
this attempt. The candidate never reached that gate, so no timed clock
readings were started. The controller recorded `timeout` at the ten-minute
gate, restored original application and saved NVS, independently read back all
five spans, reset Bench 2, and closed its journal. A separate host check found
the journal closed with `restored_and_reset`, no active custody lock, and all
five private capture-file hashes matching the journal. The owner confirmed
the usual Trail screen after restoration. Bench 1 was untouched.

Before another candidate flash, investigate the protected ProtocolInfo read
path host-side and compare candidate and original behavior without conflating
an Android callback/platform failure with a deliberate firmware rejection.

No natural stale/loss/recovery transition or approved candidate restart case
occurred. These trials do not close OT-0101e, change V1 progress, establish
radio behavior, or authorize publication. The hosted task's instruction to
report a displayed location/GNSS time is inconsistent with the target UI:
coordinates and GNSS time are not displayed, and its clock is phone-supplied.

See [preflight and host candidate](../../docs/testing/OT-0101e-TRIAL-PREFLIGHT-2026-09-25.md)
for operator pins, restoration gates, and the distinction between the current
candidate and the superseded host-only image.

## Connection diagnostic A, 2026-09-28

One owner-authorized Bench 2/S24 trial used the exact diagnostic A image
(593200 bytes, SHA-256 `4351080e407ae8bf33370737e9313ecfb8ec6865e5bb1ed076d2fd094bc85c72`)
and data-preserving V1-Test update (11974618 bytes, SHA-256
`79603e1a49b299f06908d9792658af61c85cf77bc498240693b67bb5684adec0`).
The installed package/certificate and installed APK readback matched. The S24
model was SM-S928U; Bench 2 retained its registered Heltec V4.2 identity and
US915 setting. Radio transmission was not exercised. Cases stayed closed,
batteries connected, and Bench 1 unchanged.

Original-firmware session 19 reached protected Ready. Its provisional read
returned status 0 / 20 bytes / 159 ms internal request age; authorization and
snapshot passed. The app reported display-clock sync and the owner confirmed
a physical time. The app was held stopped until passive firmware capture
was explicitly armed, then one saved-owner connection was deliberately started.
The owner observed battery 84%, GPS:6 / GPS FIX on diagnostic A.

Candidate session 21 admitted the protected read, but its exact value callback
returned Android status 133 / one byte / 5099 ms internal request age, with
1 ms dispatch and 0 ms handling. Current GATT, exact characteristic and
pending-read flags were true. No authorization or Ready followed. The owner
observed an apparent reboot during connection. The sanitized firmware capture
independently records `stack_overflow` for `ot_ble_host`, then reset and fresh
self-check/runtime startup. SDK boot reset 4 is ESP_RST_PANIC. This establishes
the candidate's BLE-task stack-overflow detection and panic reboot; neither
the exact overflowing function nor GNSS/display causality is established.

Before the fault, periodic BLE minimum free stack was 1964 bytes and app-task
minimum was 4544 bytes; these measurements can miss the deepest transient frame.
Eight callback records equal one drain batch. Raw event 34 is DATA_LEN_CHG
and event 38 is LINK_ESTAB, not authorization. Missing later callback/read
markers cannot identify the crash site because queued records may be lost
during reset. Phone and device clocks have no established subtraction anchor.

Capture ended deliberately after the first failure with `operator_stop`,
174259 ms observed of the bounded window, 34957 sanitized bytes, SHA-256
`1661f1045cb1b2533c05a6f721ab37f0e460ecd413fb34de43aee993c4ace12f`.
Early output may be missing, and its truncated-window flag remains explicit;
no firmware queue drops or overlong lines were observed. No manual retry or
profile B trial occurred.

The controller restored and independently verified original application/NVS
and all protected spans, reset Bench 2 and closed with exit 0. Separate audit
confirmed all five saved capture hashes and unchanged operator inputs. One
restored-original saved-owner reconnect reached Ready in session 22 and
reported clock sync (provisional status 0 / 20 bytes / 164 ms internal age).
The owner confirmed the usual Trail screen after restoration. No V1 credit or public
website status changed; sustained GNSS/clock/loss/recovery acceptance remains
open. Exact private result and audit are `.private/connection-a-physical-result.json`
and `.private/connection-a-restoration-audit.json` in the selected worktree.
