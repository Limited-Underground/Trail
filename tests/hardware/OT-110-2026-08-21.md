## Frozen evidence requirements

The contract requires two independently identified physical nodes and an identical, fully recorded US915 LoRa profile. Each node must bind accepted device identity, exact firmware image/source/effective-configuration/radio-adapter hashes, antenna model/gain/connector/cable-loss inputs, and applied-profile readback. The second node's exact identity and RF variant must be resolved independently before execution; no same-model or same-revision assumption is accepted. A successor recovery contract must admit exact per-node restore-route, manifest, image, partition-table, configuration, private-custody sanitized-receipt, pre-write readback, post-restore readback, and security-admission digests before any test write.

Future physical evidence must exercise the fixed eight-step sequence: off-air preflight, receiver configuration, one-byte probes in both directions, 100 frames per direction at the 163-byte `protocol_test_requirement_not_measurement`, 10 frames per direction at the measured direct ceiling, 256-byte local rejection without transmission, restart of both nodes, and 10 post-restart protocol-test frames per direction. Packet integrity/counts, latency, RSSI/SNR, close-bench distance class, timeout policy, theoretical airtime, exact profile persistence, and exact firmware/configuration identities are mandatory.
