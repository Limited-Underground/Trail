# OT-067 Protected Authorization KV Slot Media

Date: 2026-08-17

## Accepted scope

OT-067 adds one target-neutral key/value adapter beneath the accepted two-slot
authorization store. It binds only the fixed candidate partition, namespace,
and slot keys recorded by Decision 0012 and accepts only exact 32-byte
`OAP0/v0` values.

## Host evidence

The focused strict C++17 warnings-as-errors validation passes:

- 10 protected KV slot-media scenario groups; and
- the unchanged 12-group two-slot protected-store regression.

The new groups prove exact binding names, invalid-slot no-I/O behavior, absent
keys, exact reads, short and oversized refusal, typed backend failures, durable
commit requirement, post-write uncertainty, callback-reentry containment,
alternating-slot rotation, reboot restore, rollback refusal, prepared-ahead
refusal, and ambiguous-commit reconciliation through the existing coordinator.

## Exact boundary

The adapter exposes no erase, reset, repair, retry, initialization, key
provisioning, eFuse, device, or logging surface. The independent rollback floor
remains separately injected. No Heltec source, partition, sdkconfig, contract,
runtime, image, or physical device changed, and its authorization authorities
remain denied.

Historical evidence-weighted V1 remains exact 31.75% and displays as 32%.
Current V1 Companion and V2 Integrated remain unmeasured. No website status
change is required because no physical or publicly usable capability changed.
