# OT-003A Redacted Three-Device GNSS Snapshot

Date: 2026-08-12  
Evidence class: bounded connected-bench evidence  
Result: partial pass; all three GNSS paths were detected and activated, and the
packaged repeater obtained a live fix

## Purpose

Determine whether the GNSS hardware reported connected to the two assembled
Heltec bench clients and packaged SenseCAP repeater is visible to their current
MeshCore firmware. Keep coordinates, device identities, keys, PINs, addresses,
channels, and raw serial responses out of the public evidence.

## Units and installed roles

| Evidence role | Runtime board | Installed firmware/role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bench_client_1` | Heltec V4 OLED | MeshCore USB Companion `v1.16.0-07a3ca9` |
| `bench_client_2` | Heltec V4 OLED | MeshCore USB Companion `v1.16.0-07a3ca9` |
| `packaged_repeater_1` | Seeed SenseCap Solar | MeshCore Repeater `v1.16.0-07a3ca9` |

Local COM assignments were used to reach the units but are deliberately absent
from the public result because they are transient and unnecessary evidence.

## Method and privacy boundary

- The two companions were queried for MeshCore custom sensor variables. The
  upstream sensor manager exposes the `gps` variable only when serial GNSS data
  was detected during initialization.
- Both companion `gps` settings initially read `0`. The authorized bench setup
  command set each to `1`, and a second read verified the change.
- One local self-telemetry request per companion was reduced in memory to only
  whether a Cayenne LPP `gps` field was present. Coordinate values and the
  response's device prefix were neither printed nor saved.
- The repeater's documented bare `gps` command was used because it returns only
  power/status/fix/satellite count. Its initial result was `off`; the authorized
  `gps on` command enabled it. No `gps setloc`, advert-location, identity, key,
  or configuration-export command was used.
- The final read-only snapshot was produced by
  `tools/Get-MeshCoreGnssStatus.py`, whose default output omits local ports and
  all raw or identity-bearing data.

## Interpretation

- Both Heltec firmware builds detected their connected GNSS devices, powered
  them after explicit enablement, and supplied a GPS telemetry record. This is
  stronger than a purchase-listing or wiring report.
- The companion protocol has no separate privacy-safe fix/satellite status
  request. A current Heltec fix, time-to-first-fix, accuracy, stale/loss
  transition, and antenna performance are therefore still unproved here.
- The SenseCAP result proves this packaged unit's firmware/GNSS path can reach a
  live satellite fix in the present bench location. It does not establish
  accuracy, repeatable cold-start time, obstructed performance, power cost,
  solar endurance, field range, or OpenTrail target compatibility.
- GPS remained enabled on all three units at the end of the test. No radio,
  repeater, identity, channel, location-advertising, or firmware setting was
  otherwise changed.

No hardware is promoted to `validated` or `supported` by this result. Exact
received revisions, labels, physical modules/wiring, regulatory authorization,
and first-complete-client hardware remain separate gates.
