# OT-135 host-only Monocypher byte-bounded preamble correction

**HOST-ONLY CORRECTION ACCEPTED — no device access, flash, benchmark, radio,
phone operation, or execution authority occurred.**

OT-135 adds a successor capture runner for the bounded OT-133
`capture_failed` / `preamble_invalid` boundary. It does not alter OT-129 through
OT-133 or any accepted preparation, authority, adapter, receipt, or evidence.

## Corrected boundary

- Complete pre-`READY` records may contain arbitrary bytes and are ignored only
  while their cumulative complete-record length remains at or below 512 bytes.
- The former independent eight-record ceiling is removed. Because each complete
  record consumes at least its newline byte, the unchanged 512-byte budget also
  supplies a finite record-count bound without overfitting the observed nine.
- Nine fabricated complete records totaling exactly 512 bytes pass. Sixty-four
  short fabricated records inside the same budget also pass. Byte 513 fails
  `preamble_invalid`.
- Only exact complete `OTCBXCTL1 READY\n` transitions to capture. Embedded,
  prefixed, suffixed, case-changed, or CRLF lookalikes remain opaque chatter.
- A result frame before `READY`, including after nine accepted opaque records,
  remains `frame_before_ready`. Duplicate `READY` remains `ready_invalid`. Any
  opaque record after `READY` remains `frame_malformed`.
- Partial bytes, fixed deadlines, one reset/open lifecycle, exact frame count,
  the unchanged 1,014-frame parser, and the privacy-safe counter-only diagnostic
  schema remain strict.

All opaque test records are fabricated fixtures. They are not recovered device
bytes and do not establish or imply unpublished physical boot content beyond
the already accepted privacy-safe OT-133 counters.
