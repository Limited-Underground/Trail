## Scope

This acceptance adds the exact BLE Companion GATT v0 service and three
characteristic definitions to the generic `heltec_v4_bench` ESP-IDF v6.0.2
candidate. The build selects one NimBLE peripheral/GATT-server connection,
Secure Connections only, authenticated and encrypted characteristic access, a
16-byte minimum key size, no legacy pairing, and no persistent bond store.

Registration requires injected application-authorization and companion-
coordinator authority. The current application does not supply those
dependencies, register the service, initialize the NimBLE host or controller,
or advertise. Command writes are unconditionally denied before coordinator
mutation. Exact registered CCCD handles and per-connection Stream indication
subscription/disconnect ownership remain a later runtime gate; no inferred
`value_handle + 1` mapping is used.
