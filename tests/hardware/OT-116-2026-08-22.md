Monocypher lack independent candidate-specific API/configuration admission,
every included executable candidate lacks accepted retained import/build
admission, and the second measurement node lacks exact-profile admission.

execution authority false. Candidate admission, retained import/build evidence,

candidate, suite, handshake/KDF, secure-LoRa composition, or Packet V1 wire.
