## Coordinator and authority boundary

- Both displays require a visible reset/visual preflight before the authorized attempt continues.
- The coordinator requires exactly two distinct endpoints and one bound backend
  for flash and capture operations.
- Both installed Trail applications must pass exact readback and reset before a
  journal or application write.
- Writes are application-only at offset `0x10000`; benchmark readback must pass
  before capture.
- The OT-129 transport owns one pre-START reset, retries exact START until exact
  READY, starts the fixed 180-second deadline at READY, and permits no reset or
  reopen after START begins.
- Every touched node must restore, read back, and hard-reset exactly to Trail.
  Recovery-only mode does not require the benchmark artifact and remains
  retryable until restoration succeeds.
- Public results retain only anonymous roles, closed failure codes, and bounded
  counters; ports, device identifiers, filesystem paths, raw capture, and raw
  exception text remain private.
- The authority permits one attempt and is consumed on success or abort. It is
  accepted here but not executed.
