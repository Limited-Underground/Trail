# OT-017F Physical Stale-Rejection Round Trip

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: passed for two role-reversed close-bench cycles using production host
components from both repositories. This is a bounded negative-policy and radio
byte-path result, not persistent on-device state or physical authentication.

## Purpose

Prove that a physical `OGK0` rejection remains negative evidence end to end.
The public normative `OGA0` alert was accepted by the radio adapter, but the
OpenTrail ingress evaluated it against a deliberately stale UTC receive context
and produced rejected/stale through the real ACK responder. OpenGauge then had
to correlate the returned bytes without treating them as delivery success.

Success required all of the following:

- exact 64-byte alert and correlated 64-byte rejection in both endpoint roles;
- OpenTrail disposition rejected with canonical stale reason;
- OpenGauge ingress processed the correlated rejection;
- `outbox_completed=false` and zero acknowledgements;
- terminal remote-rejection evidence with no retry release;
- zero queued and in-flight entries after terminal handling; and
- zero duplicates/errors plus verified temporary-channel cleanup.

The two Heltecs each recorded exactly one sent and one received frame per cycle,
zero new core/receive errors, and empty queues. SenseCAP repeat remained on and
its errors did not increase. Endpoint cleanup passed 4/4 and no lease journal
remained. No secret, PIN, device address, or private pairing identifier is
recorded.

## Remaining gates

- Rate-limited/internal-error retryable rejection over the physical path.
- Loss, duplicate, corruption, delay, reorder, replay, restart/rebind, revoke,
  and power-loss behavior with persistent live state.
- Protected keys and transport identity bound to selected target adapters.
- Direct on-device transport plus interference, field, range, and mobility
  evidence.
