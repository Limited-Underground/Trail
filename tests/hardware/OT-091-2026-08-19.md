## Decision 0003 boundary

The contract is deliberately algorithm-neutral. The public OT-005 benchmark
plan remains `draft_blocked`; no library, suite, handshake instantiation, KDF,
packet-v1 bytes, target MTU/PHY, or deployed retry policy is selected. Existing
`OTKD/v1`, `OTCN`, and nonce-composition evidence is incorporated as an
obligation, not treated as cryptographic implementation.
