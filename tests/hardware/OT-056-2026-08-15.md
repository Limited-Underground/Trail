## Scope

OT-056 adds a fixed-memory, externally serialized runtime owner and the
ESP-IDF v6.0.2 adapter that is coded to initialize NimBLE, configure Secure
Connections/bonding security parameters, register the exact protected
companion GATT service, and advertise only standard flags plus the stable
public service UUID. Advertising-data fields contain no name, manufacturer
data, address, device/user/group identifier, or peer-derived value; the coded
own-address policy requests privacy, but BLE necessarily uses a link-layer
advertiser address and no runtime address behavior is proven.

NimBLE host callbacks cross a fixed eight-entry queue into the single
`app_main` owner context. Queue overflow, startup timeout, host reset,
advertising failure, restart exhaustion, synchronous callback reentry, and
stack-shutdown failure are terminal. Disconnect cleanup precedes one delayed,
token-correlated advertising restart; retries are capped at three.

The exact OT-054 protected-storage preflight remains
`nvs_encryption_not_configured`. The configured SC+MITM+bonding+
no-input/no-output settings and absent store do not prove or permit a usable
secure bond. Claim and normal-command authorities remain fixed-deny. Every
successful connection is immediately terminated: `BLE_HS_ENOTCONN` releases
the exact adapter session and schedules cleanup, while any other termination
error contains the owner even if no disconnect callback follows.

## Physical/runtime boundary and next gate

No target, port, controller, service, advertiser, connection, or physical
device was opened or observed. No image was erased, written, or flashed. This
is build/link and deterministic in-memory evidence for the unsupported generic
2 MB ESP32-S3 candidate, not proof the runtime works on the received board.

Before a usable claim or normal command can run, the separately accepted
protected NVS/private bond/PRF/rollback-floor gate must be provisioned and
verified. Physical target execution must then validate controller startup,
privacy-address behavior, protected characteristic permissions, pairing and
reconnect behavior, exact immediate denied-storage disconnect, bounded
re-advertising, logs, watchdog containment, and recovery on a sacrificial-first
unit.
