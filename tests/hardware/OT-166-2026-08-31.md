# OT-166 Heltec V1 durable bond-owner integration

**Date:** 2026-08-31

**Result:** Host-validated and ESP-IDF-build-linked; physical execution remains
open

## Implemented boundary

The Heltec target initializes a dedicated 12,288-byte ordinary NVS partition
without erase/retry recovery, enables NimBLE bond persistence, and installs the
`CompanionV1BondOwnerBridge` before the NimBLE host starts. The fixed 32-byte
`OTV1/v0` record is stored at `ot_v1_owner/owner_v0` and is deliberately
separate from persistent protected `OAP0` authority. The historical OAP0
preflight still runs only to require its exact fail-closed result, and its boot
self-check remains deterministic and memory-only.

The target adapter enumerates at most two entries so an unexpected extra bond
can be detected, but the application admits exactly zero or one. It derives an
opaque 128-bit reference through domain-separated PSA SHA-256 over the
authenticated Secure Connections LTK. Public addresses and raw keys do not
cross the adapter boundary and are not logged.

Boot accepts only exact empty/empty or one valid record/one matching bond.
Initial ownership requires absence, byte-exact NVS readback, and a fresh exact
post-commit inventory. Every later controller authorization takes another
fresh exact inventory. Normal commands open only after exact terminal
indication confirmation promotes that connection. Boot/session challenge and
active-duplicate rules reject altered or post-release replay. Recursive outer
GATT authority entry is latched: a new temporary lease is released before the
outer operation denies, while a preexisting exact lease remains coherent.

No automatic erase, repair, replacement, orphan cleanup, protected-media
activation, or rollback-floor claim is made.
