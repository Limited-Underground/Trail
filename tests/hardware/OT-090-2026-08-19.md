## Accepted host evidence

- Decision 0034 and `BLE_PAIRING_REPLACEMENT_V0.md` freeze the exact practical
  physical-presence pairing, saved-bond reconnect, and confirmed phone-
  replacement semantics required by Decision 0033.
- The canonical machine-readable plan is
  `tests/release-plans/OT-090-BLE-PAIRING-REPLACEMENT-CONTRACT-V0.json`.
- The contract is normally closed. Holding the designated target-neutral local
  input for at least 3000 ms and releasing it opens one exact 30-second window;
  no GPIO/button mapping is selected. It admits one candidate and generates one
  fresh locally displayed six-digit passkey per window.
- Eligible pairing is Bluetooth LE Secure Connections only, with MITM-
  authenticated passkey pairing, bonding, and an exact 16-byte/128-bit
  encryption key. Legacy,
  `Just Works`, static/debug-passkey, weaker, or ambiguous security is denied.
- New pairing is eligible only while coherently unowned and has one attempt per
  window. Ordinary reconnect rechecks the saved current bond, Secure
  Connections, MITM authentication, bonding, the 16-byte/128-bit key, a fresh
  session, and separate application authorization without rewriting ownership.
  Replacement is a separate flow whose
  candidate secure bond must be followed by a second local hold of at least
  3000 ms and release before the original 30-second deadline. The candidate
  receives no protected authority before exact candidate-owner commit and
  readback. That commit invalidates old application authorization; old-bond
  removal and verified absence precede publication of the new controller.
  Abort, expiry, interruption, or known pre-mutation failure preserves the exact
  prior owner only after candidate-bond removal and verified absence. Ambiguous
  commit, readback, candidate cleanup, or old-bond cleanup publishes neither
  controller.
- Android passkey entry is restricted to the system pairing UI; the app never
  receives, stores, displays, or logs it. The OS bond callback is bound to the
  exact candidate and transport generation, while bond state alone grants no
  application authorization. App cancel, Bluetooth permission loss, service
  stop, or disconnect closes without automatic retry.
- Timeout, mismatch, authentication/bond failure, cancellation, disconnect,
  replay, stale events, second candidates, clock failure/rollback, restart, and
  malformed or ambiguous persistence fail closed. Restart never restores a
  passkey or pending window.
- Fixed public diagnostics omit passkeys, bond keys, phone identifiers, BLE
  addresses, device-specific identifiers, private bindings, and storage
  contents.
