# OT-017E Strengthened Cross-Project Physical ACK Completion

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: passed for two role-reversed close-bench cycles using real host
components from OpenTrail and OpenGauge plus two Heltec companions and the
SenseCAP repeater. This is not persistent on-device application state,
authenticated physical key proof, a direct target transport, or a field result.

## Increment over OT-017D

OT-017D proved that exact normative `OGA0` alert bytes could pass into the real
OpenTrail ingress/responder and that its correlated `OGK0` response survived
the return radio leg. OT-017E retained those checks and additionally supplied
the returned physical ACK to an independent OpenGauge verifier built from its
production host components:

- the exact sent alert was reconstructed as a prepared and locally accepted
  `CriticalAlertOutbox` entry;
- `PeerAuthorizationRegistry` explicitly approved the trail-bridge peer;
- `CriticalAlertAckIngress` required authenticated metadata, the exact bound
  consumer boot session, replay acceptance, and exact event/condition/lifecycle
  correlation; and
- success required one acknowledgement, a completed entry, and zero queued or
  in-flight entries.

The OpenGauge verifier reconstructed state after the physical ACK returned. It
did not keep a durable live outbox during the radio wait.
