## Scope

OT-050 adds a fixed-memory, externally serialized one-connection provisional
authorization lifecycle above the accepted companion wire, coordinator, and
response-delivery gate. Its separate exact 20-byte `OTB0` v0.1 record adds the
authorization-claim capability bit and a device-issued provisional session
nonce. Protocol Info may be read only with trusted encrypted/authenticated-bond
evidence. Claim traffic additionally requires the exact registered Command,
Stream, and Stream CCCD handles, indication subscription, and ATT MTU at least
51 for the fixed 48-byte terminal record. Normal traffic remains denied until
exact Accepted/Replaced indication confirmation and requires ATT MTU 151.
Claim-capable Protocol Info also rejects a maximum fragment payload below the
mandatory 28-byte terminal payload; exact 27-reject/28-accept boundaries pass.

The lifecycle sends and confirms Pending before later exact-bound authority
resolution, allowing the local physical-presence workflow to occur. It reserves
terminal response capacity before authority mutation and promotes only after
the exact terminal connection, transport generation, session, exchange, value
handle, and delivery token are confirmed. Denial, local timeout, security loss,
unsubscribe, disconnect, congestion, negative confirmation, submission failure,
or stale callbacks cannot promote. Durable owner state may already have changed
after authority success, so post-authority delivery failure releases the live
controller lease and tombstones the transport until exact disconnect; it does
not invent a denial or delivery claim.

The build-only target composes the lifecycle with fixed fake bond, correlation,
and authority evidence plus an in-memory indication sink. The existing real
NimBLE AUTHOR callbacks remain unconditionally denied before application
authorization; they do not expose the v0.1 Protocol Info, dispatch a provisional
Command, or start/register the controller or advertiser.
