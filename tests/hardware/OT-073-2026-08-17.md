# OT-073 protected-storage source-read attempt

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: partial; one authorized read-only attempt consumed, source proof absent

## Scope

This record covers one owner-authorized, one-use attempt to collect the exact
OT-070 protected-storage source prerequisite from `OT-DEV-001`. The operation
was limited to the accepted 3,072-byte partition-table range and complete 1 MiB
source region. It granted no write, erase, reset, key, eFuse, partition
transition, recovery, or runtime-activation authority.

The device was isolated as the single matching Espressif serial device and the
owner manually placed it in ROM mode. The one-use executor enforced esptool
5.3.1, one port construction, one no-reset connection attempt, direct ESP32-S3
ROM access without a RAM stub, and only the two allowlisted read requests.

## Result

The attempt returned only the fixed sanitized result:

`OTPSTE0/v0` / `DENY-READ-FAILURE`

The authorization was consumed and the attempt was not repeated. The failure
stage is deliberately unknown because transport output, raw bytes, paths,
ports, device identifiers, and private operation/evidence identities were not
retained. Therefore neither the installed-table match nor the all-`0xFF`
source-region prerequisite is accepted.

Static review of the one-use executor confirmed that it contained no flash
write, erase, reset, key, eFuse, provisioning, or runtime-activation operation.
It created no raw output files. Both ephemeral executor versions and their
bytecode were deleted immediately after the attempt, and cleanup was verified.

## Recovery observation

With PRG released, the owner pressed RST once after the failed attempt. The
recognizable Trail logo and `BLE ADVERTISING` status returned. This is a
bounded startup/status recovery observation only; no additional ROM read,
heartbeat capture, Android scan, write, or recovery procedure was performed.

## Remaining boundary

Ready, LoRa, or GNSS. Any later physical read requires a newly reviewed
