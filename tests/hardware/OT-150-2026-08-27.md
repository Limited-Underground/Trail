# OT-150 host-only mbedTLS/PSA executable/resource bundle freeze

Date: 2026-08-27

## Purpose

Replace the non-executable OT-149 compile snapshots with a deterministic,
fixed-version candidate/control build boundary; admit the matched host-only
resource result; and freeze the exact inputs required before requesting any
new physical authority.

## Historical boundary

OT-149 successfully compiled and validated the five-operation candidate,
matched no-candidate control, strict 1,015-frame parser, and signed/zero-capable
resource successor. Its project version was not explicitly pinned. The OT-149
BIN and ELF identities therefore remain immutable historical compile-validation
snapshots and are not execution-eligible. OT-150 neither rewrites those records
nor uses those application images for a future attempt.

## Accepted host-only result

- Candidate and control are each rebuilt twice from initially absent build
  directories with exact `PROJECT_VER=ot150-mbedtls-psa-v0`, explicit
  `--no-ccache`, and offline component-manager policy.
- Each side's A/B artifact and ESP-IDF JSON2 report identities must match
  exactly. Candidate/control configuration and common artifacts remain matched;
  only the reviewed candidate-versus-no-candidate linkage differs.
- The canonical executable/resource preparation binds the exact candidate and
  control build tuples, runtime inputs, JSON2 reports, and future write
  boundary without publishing private build paths.
- The OTMRAR1 matched-resource result admits the exact host-derived linked-
  flash and static-RAM values, including signed or zero deltas without
  coercion. The canonical result and its two bound reports are authoritative
  for those generated identities and measurements.
- The runtime uses a fresh OT-150 namespace and retains the accepted exact
  START/READY, five-operation, and strict 1,015-frame boundaries.
- Only the exact candidate application bound by the canonical preparation may
  become writable after a separate authority, and only at application offset
  `0x10000`. The control application and every other artifact remain
  non-writable.
- The required restoration image is the exact OT-147 Trail application:
  `opentrail_heltec_v4_bench.bin`, 500,944 bytes, SHA-256
  `f2a58414f82eed585ba90bf7671b06c8ebfaa82e8b23f6ac2093de95143b4e0e`.

## Validation

- Focused OT-150 host suites passed 72/72: builder 8/8, executable/resource
  bundle 10/10, protocol 14/14, coordinator 11/11, execution authority 10/10,
  hardware adapter 14/14, and binding consistency 5/5.
- Matched-resource accounting tests passed 12/12.
- These are focused host-suite results; the complete host matrix has not yet
  been claimed for this closeout.
- The validator binds the exact OT-116 plan, OT-149 preparation and resource
  successor, OT-149 parser/schema, candidate/control A/B artifacts, JSON2
  reports, runtime files, and OT-147 restoration image.
- Adversarial coverage rejects A/B reuse or drift, malformed or ambiguous JSON2
  reports, candidate/control common-artifact drift, absent linkage distinction,
  wrong restoration bytes, private paths, reused/out-of-repository runtime
  bindings, authority or claim escalation, and noncanonical JSON.
- The validator has no output writer, authority builder, command runner, serial
  library, esptool integration, or device-discovery surface.

## Boundaries

OT-150 performs host builds, static validation, and matched-resource admission
only. It creates no one-attempt authority and accesses no device, endpoint, or
phone. It performs no reset, flash, benchmark capture, radio transmission, or
key/entropy operation. It does not select a candidate, library, suite,
handshake, KDF, or Packet-v1 wire format and does not complete Phase 2 or Phase
3. It adds no field-readiness, support, compatibility, regulatory, production,
end-to-end, V1-progress, or score claim.

Fresh explicit owner approval and a separately accepted authority bound to the
canonical preparation remain required before any physical attempt. No immediate
website update is required because public capability, readiness, completion
percentage, and score remain unchanged.
