## Scope

This acceptance adds a fixed-memory, target-neutral one-connection GATT
session owner. It binds exact registered Command, Stream, and CCCD handles;
ATT MTU; encrypted, authenticated, and application-authorized evidence;
indication-only subscription; coordinator session; and one outstanding
delivery token.

The indication sink must reserve the complete maximum response before the
request coordinator can mutate device authority. Congestion and guarded
reserve/submit/cancel callback re-entry reject without mutation or state
advance. Wrong or stale completion preserves the exact pending response.
Unsubscribe, security loss, submit failure, negative exact completion, or
timeout contain and block the session. A terminal tombstone blocks reuse
until the exact connection disconnects.
