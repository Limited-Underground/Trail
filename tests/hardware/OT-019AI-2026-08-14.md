## Acceptance contract

Starting from a unique fresh extraction, the verifier must:

1. recheck the manifest, every payload length and SHA-256, the inspection-only
   capability boundary, forbidden payloads, and source-free launch;
2. require the exact privacy-safe public display-name multiset on the initial
   load and every live refresh, including duplicate-sensitive comparison rather
   than set-only comparison;
3. size the packaged production window to its effective 900 x 620 minimum and
   prove that the final wrapped device card begins outside the outer viewport;
4. invoke that exact card's out-of-process UI Automation `ScrollItem` provider
   and require the card to become onscreen without selecting a device, enabling
   firmware-bundle selection, changing its public name/help, or enabling any
   Flash action;
5. repeat the established live refresh workflow three times and require the
   settled result to restore the complete public roster, empty selection, zero
   ready to flash, and disabled bundle/Flash authority;
6. after initial load, selection, and every successful refresh, require the
   current automation tree to contain no visible or assertive inspection-error
   peer and no stale public error text; and
7. remove the automation handler, close only its owned process, delete only its
   verified temporary extraction, and report zero owned-process or temporary-
   directory residue.

The independent source-free probe passed against the exact ZIP identified
above and the public roster `Heltec V4 OLED`, `SenseCAP Solar`, and
`Wio Tracker L1`.

At 96 DPI, UI Automation Transform resized the packaged production window to
exactly 900 x 620. Before the operation, the Wio card rectangle was x=425,

card's real out-of-process `ScrollItem` provider moved the rectangle to x=425,
y=254 with the same width and height and changed it to `IsOffscreen=false`.

Refresh retained keyboard focus. Selection remained zero, bounded firmware-
bundle selection remained disabled, the footer Flash action remained disabled,
Refresh remained enabled, and the exact three-found/three-runtime-identified/
zero-ready summary did not change. No `inspection-error` peer was present before
or after the transition. Cleanup reported zero owned processes and zero owned
temporary directories.

The complete `tools/Test-Host.ps1` gate passed. Windows PowerShell 5.1 parse and
