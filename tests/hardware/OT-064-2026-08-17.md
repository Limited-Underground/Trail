# OT-064 — Physical Trail OLED startup and BLE status

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: PASS — selected-unit startup/status OLED evidence; not supported hardware

## Scope

Only `OT-DEV-001` participated. `OT-DEV-002` remained excluded and untouched.
The owner authorized exactly one factory-application update at `0x010000`; no
full-chip erase, bootloader, partition-table, OTA-data, recovery, or unit-2
write was authorized or performed.

The target-local display path uses the documented Heltec V4-family candidate
binding: 128 x 64 one-bit SSD1315-compatible OLED, I2C address `0x3c`, SDA GPIO
17, SCL GPIO 18, reset GPIO 21, and Vext control GPIO 36 enabled low. Physical
acceptance proves this binding drove the enclosed selected unit; it does not
identify the exact controller die, exact received board revision, or generalize
support to another unit.

## Write and verification

Pinned esptool 5.3.1 rechecked the exact application and one-attempt
configuration before execution. One write invocation updated only the factory
application at `0x010000`. The 470,928-byte logical image ends at `0x082f90`;
its automatic sector erase ends at `0x083000`, within the factory partition
ending at `0x500000`.

The first standalone read-only verification completed without retained raw
output, but its `Verification successful.` wording was rejected by the wrapper's
too-narrow success parser. An offline check of the pinned esptool source
identified that parser defect. One corrected standalone read-only verification
then passed. There was no second write, erase, reset, or recovery attempt. Raw
port, MAC, and device identifiers were neither displayed nor retained.

## Physical acceptance

Only after the passing verification, the owner pressed `RST` once with `PRG`
released and observed:

- a recognizable, readable, correctly oriented Limited Underground Trail
  startup logo;
- a subsequent truthful `BLE ADVERTISING` display state.

A privacy-safe 16-second USB serial observation then found the boot self-check
PASS marker, four heartbeat records, and no self-check failure, runtime failure,
panic, abort, or Guru Meditation marker. Raw logs were discarded.

The Android client's exact-service scan reported exactly one compatible nearby
device. Nothing was selected, connected, or paired, and no address, identifier,
or unrelated scan result was retained.

- A final post-record incremental pinned build reproduced the accepted
  470,928-byte application hash. Its expanded 5,014-byte build-only receipt has
  SHA-256 `2E898801AF0C7E2EB16CCCE68DA54516B060A2FCEBC5F706C55FFBDC047350FF`;
  build-only `NOT-FLASHED` fields describe that helper run, not the physical gate.
