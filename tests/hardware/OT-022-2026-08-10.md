# OT-022 Privacy-Safe Three-Radio Burst Evidence

Date: 2026-08-10

## Scope

Exercise the existing two-client-plus-repeater MeshCore bench at a materially
faster cadence than the five-hour soak, then prove that the raw result can be
reduced to a machine-validated public record without transport ports, hardware
identifiers, channel names, coordinates, or secrets.

This is a bounded close-bench burst and logging test. It is not a four- or
eight-person field test and does not prove that the repeater was required.

## Hardware and configuration

- two Heltec V4 OLED USB Companion nodes;
- one Seeed SenseCAP Solar MeshCore repeater;
- MeshCore `v1.16.0-07a3ca9` on all three roles;
- USA/Canada profile: 910.525 MHz, 62.5 kHz, SF7, CR5;
- client transmit power 10 dBm and repeater transmit power 22 dBm;
- antennas previously user-confirmed attached; and
- repeater enabled before and after the run.

Read-only client preflight immediately before the test showed matching model,
firmware, and radio settings, full/near-full battery voltage, empty queues, and
zero core/receive errors.

## Procedure

- A recoverable exact-name lease created one temporary private channel on both
  clients without persisting its secret.
- Clients alternated one application marker every two seconds for one requested
  minute.
- The repeater was observed through counter deltas.
- The script erased the leased channel only after exact-name verification on
  each client and removed the lease journal only after both cleanups passed.
- The raw JSON remained in ignored local build state. The public summary was
  generated through the `OTFL0` converter and independently validated.

## Interpretation and limits

- The burst adds a faster-cadence regression to the earlier low-duty and
  five-hour results; it does not replace either.
- Exact +30 repeater flood RX/TX is participation evidence. Close-bench flooding
  does not establish that delivery depended on the repeater; OT-009's forced
  one-hop negative control remains the repeater-required evidence.
- Companion receive/flood counters include MeshCore protocol behavior and are
  not one-to-one application delivery counts.
- Messages were MeshCore temporary-channel markers, not authenticated OpenTrail
  packet-v1 traffic.
- No field range, motion, interference, battery endurance, regulatory, four-node,
  or eight-node conclusion is claimed.
