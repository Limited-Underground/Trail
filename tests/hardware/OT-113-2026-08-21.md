## Contract coverage

- Ordered non-TX preflight and receiver configuration remain mandatory.
- Per direction counts remain `1, 100, 10, reject, restart, 10`.
- The raw probe is exactly one byte `a5`.
- `OTD1` has an exact 16-byte header; total wire sizes are 163 and 255.
- A request for 256 total wire bytes must reject locally with no TX receipt.
- A bounded 16-byte `OTA1` acknowledgement closes each valid DATA exchange.
- Machine receipts reconcile session, direction, sequence, DATA and ACK hashes.
- Acceptance requires zero lost, duplicate, corrupt, or unexpected frames.
- RSSI, SNR, exact-airtime timeout inputs, and end-to-end protocol RTT are
  recorded, including responder turnaround.
- Exact successful command-chain and receiver-start receipts plus OTA exchange
  before and after restart prove the applied command profile only.

## Supersession and recovery boundary

OT-113 replaces only the prior execution preconditions that demanded unavailable
exact antenna/FCC inputs, a successor recovery contract, mandatory prior-state
restoration, and register/calibrated RF readback. Historical OT-110 and OT-112
evidence remains intact.

These are disposable bench nodes. ROM bootloader recovery plus committed source
and the exact built image is sufficient. MeshCore and prior diagnostic state do
not need to be preserved or restored, and diagnostic firmware may remain after
the run. Owner confirmation may separately establish bounded device/build/flash/
low-power close-bench transmit authority and peer identity without publishing
device identifiers.

## Boundary

This host-only contract creates no `OTRPE1` physical evidence and no `OTRPA1`
admission. It does not close the direct-radio requirement or advance readiness.
It makes no antenna-gain, EIRP, FCC/regulatory, support, range, secure-LoRa,
Packet V1, benchmark, selection, or score claim. Future evidence and admission
must each bind the contract and evidence by the exact raw and canonical hashes;
even successful admission requires a successor readiness decision and new
executable benchmark plan.
